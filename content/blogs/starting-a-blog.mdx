Until now this site has been a portfolio: a hero, three projects, a contact
form. Static, tidy, and not a place where I could think out loud.

A blog fixes the second part. Most of what I learn doesn't belong in a
project README — it belongs in a short post that someone else might search
for at 1am.

## What I'll write about

Roughly three things:

- **Building in public** — decisions from CampusZen and Kivo, including the
  ones I got wrong.
- **The self-taught path** — what I'm studying, what I skipped, and what
  actually stuck.
- **Application security** — the space I want to work in, written as I learn
  it rather than after I've mastered it.

## How a post gets published

There's no CMS and no admin panel. A post is a single file dropped into
`content/blogs/` with a little frontmatter at the top. The filename becomes
the URL, the frontmatter becomes the title, date and tags, and everything
below it is just Markdown.

So publishing is: write the file, run the dev server, look at it, commit.
Nothing in `src/` changes — that's the whole point of setting it up this way.
